Healthy Buddha Bowl

A nutritious and colorful bowl packed with fresh vegetables, quinoa, and healthy fats. Perfect for a quick and easy lunch or dinner!

⏱️
30 min
Total Time
👥
2 servings
Servings
📊
Easy
Difficulty

💡 Chef's Tip

You can meal-prep this recipe by storing the ingredients separately and assembling just before eating to keep everything fresh and crispy!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fresh spinach
  • 1 cup quinoa, cooked
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup chickpeas, roasted
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

1

Cook quinoa according to package instructions and let it cool.

2

Wash and prepare all fresh vegetables. Slice the avocado and halve the cherry tomatoes.

3

In a large bowl, arrange the spinach as a base.

4

Add cooked quinoa, avocado slices, cherry tomatoes, and roasted chickpeas on top.

5

Drizzle with olive oil and fresh lemon juice.

6

Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve immediately and enjoy!
